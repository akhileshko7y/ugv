# Learning path: Cloud-Teleoperated UGV Arena

Drive a physical rover over the internet from a browser tab, with sub-120ms round-trip latency.

Self-paced course of short **primers**. Read a lesson, then take its **Check
yourself** quiz: commit to an answer in your head, then click *Reveal* to check
and learn why. Nothing is scored or tracked — this is for your own understanding.
Each lesson covers the basics; follow the pointers to explore deeper on your own.

## Reading order

1. [Overview](00-overview.md) — what it does and why *(product)*
2. [Architecture](01-architecture.md) — how it works *(code)*
3. [Extending](02-extending.md) — how to change it *(code)*
4. [Operations](03-operations.md) — run, deploy, debug *(operations)*
5. [Glossary](04-glossary.md) — domain terms *(reference)*
